What's on the floor

Everything,
in one room.

Most gyms in the region buy machines because machines are easy. WAKA bought racks because that is what people actually need — and then hired coaches to teach people how to use them properly.

A standard session at any WAKA includes a warm-up area, a strength zone with platforms and racks, a functional rig for suspension and gymnastic work, dumbbells and kettlebells up to heavy, and a cardio floor that is not an afterthought.

Online through the WAKA portal, or in person at the club — walk in and settle at the front desk. Mobile money, card and cash are all accepted. No prior booking required.

Practical

Before your
first session.

What classes does WAKA run?

Functional fitness and CrossFit-style sessions, strength and conditioning classes, and the WAKA bootcamp — a high-energy conditioning session that runs most mornings at 06:30. Class times are posted at each club and in the member portal.

Is there personal training at WAKA?

Yes. Every WAKA club has coaches available for one-to-one and small-group training. Sessions are booked directly with the club — call WAKA Kimihurura on 0794 106 015, WAKA Town on 0785 379 577, or WAKA Kibagabaga on 0788 797 009.

Can beginners train at WAKA?

Yes. The bootcamp and group classes are scaled for all levels, and coaches will walk you through the movements before a session. If you have never used a barbell, start with a coached session.

Does WAKA have a climbing wall?

Yes — the bouldering wall is at WAKA Town downtown. It is a low indoor wall rather than a full climbing gym, so check at the desk that it is open before making a special trip.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

No. Bars, plates, dumbbells, kettlebells, rigs and cardio machines are all provided. Bring shoes, a towel and a water bottle — water is available to buy at the café.
