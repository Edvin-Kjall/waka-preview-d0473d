Coworking

The gym comes
with the desk.

The first lifestyle hub of its kind in Africa: a coworking floor in downtown Kigali with the gym included in every work membership.

WAKA Town downtownHot desksPrivate officesGym included

The idea

A desk next
to a barbell.

WAKA Town was built on a simple observation: the people who work hardest in Kigali are also the ones least likely to find time for a separate gym trip. So we removed the trip.

You can take a call on the fifth floor, walk forty metres, and be under a bar. Or train at 06:30, shower, and be at your desk before nine. Members describe it as the only place in the city where a lunch break can be a workout.

Practical

Coworking
questions.

Does a coworking membership include the gym?

Yes. Every WAKA work membership — hot desk, semi-private desk or private office — includes full gym access for the member. It is the reason WAKA exists as a combined club.

What coworking options are available?

Three: hot desks in the shared space, semi-private desks for people who want a regular spot, and private offices for teams. Meeting rooms are bookable by the hour.

Where is the WAKA coworking space?

At WAKA Town, on the 5th floor of the Makuza Parking Building in downtown Kigali (Nyarugenge), behind Simba Supermarket.

Is there internet and a café?

Yes — the coworking floor has business WiFi and there is a café in the building serving coffee from early morning into the afternoon.

Can I book a meeting room or event space?

Yes. Meeting rooms and the wider event space at WAKA Town are available to members and to external bookings. Contact info@wakaglobal.com with dates and numbers.
