03

Questions

Membership
questions.

How much is a WAKA membership?

Current prices are published in the WAKA portal, and the front desk will quote you in RWF on the spot. As a guide, a day pass has historically been around $8 and monthly gym membership from around RWF 28,000. Annual memberships work out cheaper than twelve months paid monthly.

Can I pay at the club instead of online?

Yes. You can join or renew online through the WAKA portal, or walk into any club and pay at the front desk with mobile money, card or cash. No prior booking is needed and there is no penalty for paying in person.

What is the difference between monthly and annual?

A monthly membership is tied to your home club and renews each month. An annual membership covers all three WAKA clubs and is paid once for twelve months, which works out roughly two months cheaper than paying monthly.

Do work memberships include the gym?

Always. Every hot desk, semi-private desk and private office membership at WAKA Town includes full gym access for the member.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership?

Monthly memberships can be cancelled with notice before the next renewal. Freezes are considered case by case — speak to the front desk or email info@wakaglobal.com.

Do you offer corporate or team memberships?

Yes. WAKA builds corporate plans that bundle desks and gym access for teams, with consolidated invoicing. Email info@wakaglobal.com with your headcount.

Is there a joining fee?

There is no induction or joining fee. You pay for the membership and, if you want it, coaching.
