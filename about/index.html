About

Twelve years
of building
the room.

WAKA started in 2014 with a complaint — that the gyms in Kigali were not worth the walk — and a stubborn belief that a club is a community before it is a facility.

March 2014 founded03 clubsKigali RwandaFitness & coworking

The founding idea

"Equipment in
a room does not
make a club."

WAKA opened in March 2014, founded by Jeannetta, Dennis Dybdal, Jon Wagner and Miruka Martin. The founding team had looked around Kigali and the wider region and found the same thing everywhere: rooms full of equipment, and almost nothing that made you want to come back.

Community matters. Education matters. Good customer service matters. That was the argument in 2014 and it is still the argument now — from how the front desk greets you to what you find in the locker room. The stated goal was to build a brand that represents quality, world-class design and a 360-degree approach to health, fitness and lifestyle, at a price a young professional in Kigali can actually afford.

Then came the second idea: if people have to choose between work and training, they will choose work. So WAKA Town put both on the same floor — the first lifestyle hub of its kind in Africa, where the gym comes free with the desk.

01

Timeline

One club
at a time.

2014

WAKA Kimihurura opens

March 2014. Four founders, one strength room on KG 674 Street, and a customer-service standard imported from outside the gym industry.

2018

WAKA Town — the lifestyle hub

Downtown Kigali, fifth floor of the Makuza parking building. Gym, coworking, café and event space in one building — the first of its kind in Africa, and the moment WAKA stopped being only a gym.

2019

Hikes and community

The hike programme begins: buses leaving Kimihurura before dawn, taking members onto Rwandan ridgelines they had never walked.

2023

The first FITTEST

A competition hosted inside WAKA's own gym, with a modest field and a big idea: Rwanda should have a proper functional-fitness championship.

2024–25

Kibagabaga joins

Formerly Cali Fitness, the Kibagabaga club joins the network — bringing a neighbourhood crowd, the earliest opening hours, and free parking.

2026

The FITTEST goes international

The third edition moves to Zaria Court with a 16.6 million RWF prize pool and athletes from six countries. Roughly 40% of the field is women.

Leadership

"Yesterday's
version of
ourselves."

Ivan Munyengango, WAKA's founder and chief executive, describes the championship as a vehicle for something larger than sport: healthier communities, young athletes who can compete internationally, and a career path in coaching, physiotherapy, nutrition, sports medicine, event management and media.

"WAKA The FITTEST was never meant to be just another competition," he says. "Our biggest competition is not another country; it is yesterday's version of ourselves."

02

The founders

Four people,
one complaint.

Co-founder

Jeannetta

Moved to Kigali from New York in 2012 to work with the Government of Rwanda's public-private partnership unit. Had never run a business, and — in her words — felt no desire to.

Co-founder

Dennis Dybdal

Ran an e-learning business, Green Zebra, before WAKA. Brought the operational discipline that turned a good idea into three clubs.

Co-founder

Jon Wagner

Founder and former CEO of Nairobi Java House. Understood better than anyone on the team what hospitality actually means at scale.

Co-founder

Miruka Martin

Founder and CEO of Kava Africa. The local operator's eye for property, people and how a room should feel.

03

What we believe

Four rules,
mostly.

01

The welcome is the product

What happens at the front desk in the first thirty seconds decides whether someone comes back. It is the cheapest thing to get right and the easiest to get wrong.

02

Equipment is the floor, not the ceiling

Good bars and good racks are the minimum. Coaching, programming and the people around you are what make a session worth the trip.

03

Work and training are not rivals

Put them in the same building and both get better. That observation built WAKA Town and it has held for eight years.

04

Affordable is a design decision

Quality at a price local professionals can sustain. Every design and staffing choice is measured against that, not against what a gym in London would charge.
