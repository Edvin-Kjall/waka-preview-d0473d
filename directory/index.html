The building

One address,
a whole
arcade.

M Peace Plaza — known across Kigali as Kwa Makuza — is a 29,383 m² mixed-use complex in the central business district: retail outlets, office space, apartments and more than five pairs of lifts. WAKA Town occupies the fifth floor.

Because so much sits under one roof, people arrive for a haircut and leave with a suit, a flight rebooked and a training session booked. The directory below is for exactly that.

Practical

Finding your
way around.

Where is WAKA Town inside the building?

WAKA Town is on the 5th floor of M Peace Plaza — known locally as Kwa Makuza — in downtown Kigali, Nyarugenge. Take the lift from the ground-floor lobby; the club is behind Simba Supermarket in the CBD.

What businesses are in the same building as WAKA Town?

M Peace Plaza is a 29,383 m² mixed-use complex with retail outlets, offices and apartments. Named tenants have included LG Resto & Casino, Lamane Bakery, Camelia, Ian Boutique, B Lita Store and a Kenya Airways booking office, alongside craft stores, electronics shops, salons and travel agencies.

Do I need a WAKA membership to visit the building?

No. The ground-floor retail arcade, restaurants and shops are open to the public. A membership is only needed for the WAKA floor — and a day pass covers that if you just want to try it.

Is there parking at WAKA Town?

Yes. M Peace Plaza has its own multi-storey parking, which is why the club's address is given as the Makuza parking building. More than five pairs of lifts serve the complex.

Can I rent office space in WAKA Town?

Yes — hot desks, semi-private desks and private offices are available on the WAKA floor, with the gym included. For a retail unit elsewhere in the plaza, contact the building directly.
