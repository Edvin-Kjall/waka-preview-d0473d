The club

WAKA Kimihurura

WAKA opened here in March 2014, in Kimihurura, because the founders could not find a gym in Kigali they wanted to go to. The brief was simple: good equipment, good light, good people, and a front desk that treats you like a member rather than a transaction.

Twelve years on it is still the club most members call home. It has kept its character — Olympic bars, bumper plates, kettlebells, a proper cardio floor — while adding the coaching and classes that the rest of the network is known for. Every WAKA hike departs from this door at 04:30, which tells you something about the crowd.

Getting there

Papyrus House on KG 674 Street, in the heart of Kimihurura — a short walk from KIM Hotel and surrounded by the restaurants and embassies the neighbourhood is known for.

From the city centre it is a ten-minute drive. There is street parking along KG 674, and the club is an easy moto ride from Kacyiru, Kiyovu and Nyarutarama.

Nearby: Kimihurura, Kacyiru, Kiyovu, KIM Hotel.

Practical

Before you come.

What are WAKA Kimihurura's opening hours?

Monday to Friday 05:30–22:00, and 08:00–21:00 at the weekend.

Where do WAKA hikes leave from?

WAKA Kimihurura — Papyrus House, 18 KG 674 St. Buses depart at 04:30 and the group returns the same afternoon.

Is WAKA Kimihurura the same membership as the other clubs?

An annual WAKA membership covers all three clubs. Monthly memberships are tied to a home club, so tell the front desk which door you will mostly walk through.

Can I train with a coach at Kimihurura?

Yes. Personal training and small-group coaching are booked directly with the club on 0794 106 015.
