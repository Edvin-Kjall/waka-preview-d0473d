The club

WAKA Town

WAKA Town is the club that turned WAKA from a gym into a lifestyle hub. When it opened it was described — by its own founders — as the first of its kind in Africa: a place where you can work, exercise, meet, grow and get inspired without leaving the building.

It sits five floors up on the Makuza parking building, which means you arrive to a view over Nyarugenge rather than a basement. Mornings belong to the bootcamp crowd, lunchtime to laptops, and the late afternoon to the weight room. The café is the hinge between all three.

Getting there

The club is on the 5th floor of the Makuza parking building, behind Simba Supermarket in the central business district. Take the lift from the ground-floor lobby.

If you are driving, park in the Makuza building itself. Coming from Kiyovu or Nyarugenge you are inside ten minutes; from Kimihurura, budget fifteen to twenty depending on the hour. There is no shortage of moto and taxi options on the street below.

Nearby: Kiyovu, Park Inn, Makuza Peace Plaza, Nyarugenge, CBD.

In the building

One address,
a whole arcade.

WAKA Town occupies the fifth floor of M Peace Plaza — Kwa Makuza — a 29,383 m² complex with a bakery, a casino restaurant, tailors, a Kenya Airways office and a floor of shops beneath you.

Two ways to pay

Online through the WAKA portal, or in person at the club — walk in and settle at the front desk. Mobile money, card and cash are all accepted. No prior booking required.

Practical

Before you come.

What are WAKA Town's opening hours?

Monday to Friday 05:30–22:00, and 08:00–21:00 on Saturday and Sunday.

Is there parking at WAKA Town?

Yes — the club sits inside the Makuza parking building, so parking is in the same structure. Take the lift to the 5th floor.

Can I work at WAKA Town without a membership?

Yes. Day passes cover the gym, and coworking day passes are available at the front desk. Every work membership also includes full gym access.

Where is WAKA Town exactly?

Makuza Parking Building, 5th Floor, in Nyarugenge — behind Simba Supermarket in Kigali's central business district.
