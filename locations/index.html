Questions

Choosing a club.

How many WAKA clubs are there in Kigali?

Three: WAKA Town downtown on the 5th floor of the Makuza Parking Building, WAKA Kimihurura at Papyrus House on KG 674 Street, and WAKA Kibagabaga on KG 19 Avenue.

Does one membership cover all three clubs?

An annual WAKA membership gives access to all three clubs. Monthly memberships are tied to a home club — you can train at the others for a small supplement, or upgrade at any time.

Which club has coworking?

WAKA Town downtown is the coworking club. It has hot desks, semi-private desks, private offices and meeting rooms, plus a café. Gym access is included with every work membership.

What time do WAKA clubs open?

WAKA Town and WAKA Kimihurura open at 05:30 on weekdays and 08:00 at weekends. WAKA Kibagabaga opens at 05:00 on weekdays and 06:00 at weekends.
