WAKA The FITTEST · 3rd edition

The
FITTEST.

Rwanda's national functional-fitness championship. In 2026 it drew more than a hundred athletes from six countries to Zaria Court — and put 16.6 million RWF on the floor.

What began three years ago as a competition hosted inside a local gym has become one of East and Central Africa's leading functional fitness events — attracting elite and amateur competitors, and putting Rwandan athletes on international start lines.

The 2026 field brought athletes from Rwanda, Kenya, Uganda, Canada, the United States and the Philippines. Organisers ran a dedicated athletes' recovery lounge with hydration, nutritional support and recovery services — a first for the event.

02

Categories

Five fields.
Three divisions.

Standards are enforced. Movement standards for each edition are published well in advance, and division selection is final once payment is confirmed.

ELITE

Women Elite

ELITE

SCALED

SCALED

Men Scaled

The scaled men's field — a serious competition with standards a committed athlete can reach.

TEAM

Team of 4

Mixed or same-gender teams of four. Coordinated execution, load sharing, cumulative scoring.

03

Format

How the day
is structured.

Competition runs across three scored events. The combination is built to test strength, engine, skill, strategy and recovery management — not just one quality.

Before the day

Athlete check-in happens the day before competition. Waivers are signed, divisions confirmed, wristbands issued, and the mandatory athlete briefing held. There is no check-in on competition morning, and no programming changes are made on the day.

During the day

Heats run from 09:00. Leaderboards update live, and results are verified before any awards are presented. A designated warm-up zone is available throughout for competing athletes, coaches and authorised staff.

Judging and protests

Judging follows published movement standards. Athletes may submit a formal protest within the defined window after their heat, through the Head Judge. Decisions are final after review.

04

Questions

Everything
athletes ask.

When is WAKA The FITTEST?

The 2026 edition was held on Saturday 1 August 2026 at Zaria Court in Kigali, starting at 09:00. The fourth edition is expected in 2027; the date is announced on Instagram and through the FITTEST site.

What divisions and categories are there?

Three divisions across five categories: Women Elite, Men Elite, Women Scaled, Men Scaled and Team of 4. Elite is the highest performance tier with advanced movements and heavier loads; Scaled is competitive but with moderately challenging movements; Team of 4 is mixed or same-gender teams of four with cumulative scoring.

How much prize money is there?

The 2026 edition carried a prize pool of 16,600,000 RWF, distributed across divisions and placement tiers.

Who can compete?

Anyone who meets the published division standards. The 2026 field drew more than a hundred athletes from six countries — Rwanda, Kenya, Uganda, Canada, the United States and the Philippines — with roughly 40% women.

How does the competition work?

There are three scored events across the day, designed to test strength, engine, skill, strategy and recovery management. Movement standards are released in advance and do not change on competition day. Scoring is points-based with live leaderboards, verified before awards.

When is athlete check-in?

The day before competition. Waivers are signed, divisions confirmed, wristbands issued and the mandatory athlete briefing held. There is no check-in on competition morning.

Can spectators attend?

Yes — the competition floor at Zaria Court is built for spectators, with seating and full event infrastructure. Access details are announced before each edition.

Where is Zaria Court?

Zaria Court is an outdoor sports and event venue in Kigali, Rwanda, with a spacious competition area, professional-grade equipment and spectator seating.
