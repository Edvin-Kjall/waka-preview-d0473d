Practical

Event questions.

How do I buy tickets for WAKA events?

Event tickets are sold through the WAKA tickets platform at events.fitbase.rw. Some events — including hikes — can also be paid for by scanning a MoPay QR code, or in person at the club.

What events does WAKA run?

Two programmes dominate: WAKA Hikes, guided day hikes across Rwanda departing from WAKA Kimihurura, and WAKA The FITTEST, the national functional-fitness championship held each August at Zaria Court in Kigali.

Do I need to be a WAKA member to join an event?

No. Hikes and WAKA The FITTEST are open to anyone. Members get priority booking and member rates on most events.

Where do hikes depart from?

Every hike leaves from WAKA Kimihurura — Papyrus House, 18 KG 674 St — at 04:30, and returns the same afternoon.
