Why we exist

We built the
place we
wanted to
train in.

In 2014 the gyms in Kigali weren't worth the walk. So we opened one — and then did the thing nobody else was doing: we put a workspace beside it.

Equipment in a room does not make a club. Community does. Education does. Being greeted by name does. That was the founding observation, and it has not really changed in twelve years.

What has changed is the scale. Three addresses. A full coworking floor downtown. A hiking programme that sells out. And a national championship that now pulls athletes from six countries into Kigali for a single weekend in August.

Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Where is WAKA in Kigali?

WAKA operates three clubs in Kigali: WAKA Town on the 5th floor of the Makuza Parking Building downtown (Nyarugenge), WAKA Kimihurura at Papyrus House, 18 KG 674 St, and WAKA Kibagabaga on KG 19 Avenue in Gasabo.

Can I pay at the club instead of online?

Yes. You can join or renew online through the WAKA portal, or simply walk into any club and pay at the front desk with mobile money, card or cash. No prior booking is needed.

Do I need a membership to try the gym?

No. Day passes are available at every WAKA club, so you can train once before committing to a monthly or annual membership.

Is the gym included in a coworking membership?

Yes. Every WAKA work membership — hot desk, semi-private desk or private office — includes full gym access.
